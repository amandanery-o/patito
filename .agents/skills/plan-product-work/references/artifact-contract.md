# Product artifact contract

## PRD

Include: context, problem, audiences, goals, non-goals, requirements with `REQ-NNN` IDs, user journeys, business rules, data and permissions, offline/sync behavior, accessibility, success measures, rollout, risks, assumptions, and open questions.

## Feature

Use filename `FNNN-short-slug.md`. Include: linked requirements, outcome, actors, scope, flow, business rules, data changes, permissions, offline behavior, acceptance criteria, edge cases, observability, rollout, dependencies, non-goals, and open questions.

## Task

Use `TASK-NNN` IDs. Include: linked feature, objective, expected files or system area, implementation notes, tests, completion checks, dependencies, risk, and status. A task must be independently reviewable and must not rely on vague completion language.

## Traceability

Maintain a table with columns: Requirement, Feature, Tasks, Acceptance coverage, Status. Never reuse or renumber published IDs.
